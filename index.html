Straight answers to common questions.

What is an annuity, in plain English?

An annuity is a contract with an insurance company. You put money in, and the company agrees to specific terms in return, such as paying you income for a set number of years or for the rest of your life. The terms are written in the contract. Guarantees depend on the financial strength and claims-paying ability of the insurance company. Annuities work in different ways, so Randy starts with what you need your money to do.

What is a Roth conversion?

It means moving money from a traditional retirement account into a Roth, which changes how that money is taxed later. It isn't right for everyone, and timing matters. Randy can show you where you stand today. For tax advice, talk with your tax professional.

Do I still need life insurance if my kids are grown?

It depends on who still counts on your income, what you want to leave behind, and how you want to cover final expenses or care costs. Some people need less coverage later in life. Others use life insurance to pass on what they have built. Randy helps you sort out which describes you.

Why would someone own gold or silver?

Some people like owning something physical that isn't tied to a company or a bank account. Metals can also add variety to a plan. They can go up and down in price like anything else, and you pay a premium over the spot price when you buy. Randy walks you through both sides.
